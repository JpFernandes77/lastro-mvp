# Limitations & Claims Discipline

## Claims supported by the current MVP

The project can currently demonstrate:

- structured evidence ingestion;
- provenance-aware evidence processing;
- AI-assisted interpretation;
- deterministic verification of explicit criteria;
- independent verification mechanisms;
- Consensus Core agreement for the covered scenario;
- bounded competency state transitions;
- human adjudication as an exception path;
- deterministic state records;
- Solana Devnet integrity-anchoring capability;
- verification of an anchored record when a current Devnet transaction is available.

## Claims that remain hypotheses

The following are intentionally not presented as validated facts:

- the definitive buyer;
- willingness to pay;
- pricing;
- recurring commercial adoption;
- quantitative economic impact;
- superiority over existing organizational workflows;
- Dynamic Role Architecture as a validated commercial wedge;
- universal competency taxonomy;
- universal suitability of automated competency decisions.

## Market validation boundary

Public market statistics can support the existence or relevance of a problem, but they do not establish customer demand for this product.

Likewise:

- an interview opinion is not willingness to pay;
- a demo is not a pilot;
- a pilot is not recurring adoption;
- public trend data is not proof that this architecture is required.

The next validation layer therefore concerns real organizations, real workflows, real evidence, and observable behavior.

## Safety and governance boundary

The project does not intend to:

- autonomously hire or fire people;
- replace all human assessment;
- assign a universal human-worth score;
- put sensitive learning data on-chain;
- treat AI output as sufficient authority for competency state;
- present blockchain as a truth oracle.

## Research status

Dynamic Role Architecture remains a research direction:

```
Organizational Data
   ↓
Evidence / Competency Graph
   ↓
Current Competency State
   ↓
Work Change
   ↓
Competency Gap
   ↓
Emerging Role Candidate
   ↓
Requalification
   ↓
Proof of Competency
```

Any emerging role should be evidence-supported and subject to the same verification and governance discipline; human adjudication remains an exception when the verification process cannot resolve a material conflict, rather than being invented by an LLM.

## Guiding principle

> **The system should be able to say “we do not know” as structurally as it can say “we have evidence.”**
